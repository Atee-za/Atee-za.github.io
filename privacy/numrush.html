Numrush Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Publisher: Zono Games · Contact: athenkosizono@gmail.com

Numrush is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. In short: we have no accounts and no servers of our own. Your game progress stays on your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and the optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your settings (sound, vibration, hints), level stars and best times, daily puzzle results and streak, and whether you bought "Remove Ads". This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system version and language;
  • ad interactions, such as ad views and taps, and app performance diagnostics.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android device settings.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing details. Google Play tells the app only whether your Google account owns the purchase, so it can be restored on a new device or after a reinstall.

Sharing your score

If you tap "Share", the app opens your device's share menu with a short text such as your daily time. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not run our own analytics or crash reporting, and we do not sell your data.
  • Data sent to Google by the SDKs above is encrypted in transit.

Children

Numrush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you have concerns, contact us at the address below.

Your choices and requests

Because we keep no data about you on our own servers, there is nothing for us to access or delete. You can delete the app's local data by uninstalling it. For data held by Google, use your Google account settings or the links above. For any question about this policy, email athenkosizono@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
