YO Player Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Publisher: Zono Games · Contact: athenkosizono@gmail.com

YO Player is a music player for Android that plays the songs already on your phone. It can also back up your own music to your own Google Drive. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. In short: we have no accounts and no servers of our own, there are no ads or analytics, and nothing leaves your phone unless you back it up to your own Google Drive.

Music on your phone

With your permission ("Music and audio" on Android 13 and later, "Files and media" on older versions), the app reads the audio files on your device so it can list and play them, and shows their titles, artists, albums and cover art. Your music is never sent to us.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your settings (theme, equalizer, sleep timer), your playlists, a play history used for "Recently played" and "Most played", your playback queue, a list of your Google Drive backups, and whether you own YO Player Pro. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. Android may include it in your device's own backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps; you control that in your Android backup settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from the phone.

Google Drive backup (optional)

Backup is off until you tap Connect Google Drive and sign in with a Google account of your choice. Then:

  • What the app can access. The app asks only for the Google Drive permission drive.file. It can see and manage only the files and folders that YO Player itself creates in your Drive. It cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • What is sent, and where. When you back up a song, the audio file goes from your phone directly to a "YO Player" folder in your own Google Drive, together with the song's title, artist and album so it can be shown and restored later. It travels over an encrypted connection to Google. We never receive your songs, and they are never stored on a server we control.
  • Your account. The app shows the email address of the connected Google account in the Backup tab. It is stored on your device only.
  • Restoring. When you restore a song, it is downloaded from your Drive into the Music/YO Player folder on your phone.
  • Deleting and disconnecting. You can delete backups in the app (swipe a song left, or select songs and tap Delete); they move to your Google Drive trash. Disconnect signs you out and removes the app's access to your Drive. Your backed-up files stay in your Drive until you delete them there. You can also remove YO Player's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

YO Player's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to back up and restore your music at your request; it is not used for advertising, not sold, and not read by people.

Back up only music that you have the right to copy. Google stores your Drive files under the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase (YO Player Pro)

Free use includes a few backups; YO Player Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited backup. It is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing details. Google Play tells the app only whether your Google account owns Pro, so it can be restored on a new device or after a reinstall. Playing music, restoring your backups, folders and deleting are always free.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads and do not use any advertising SDK.
  • We do not run analytics or crash reporting, and we do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, photos, videos, location or messages.

Children

YO Player is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you have concerns, contact us at the address below.

Your choices and requests

Because we keep no data about you on our own servers, there is nothing for us to access or delete. You can delete your backups in the app or in Google Drive, disconnect Google Drive in the app, and delete the app's local data by uninstalling it. For data held by Google, use your Google account settings. For any question about this policy, email athenkosizono@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
